Community
Guidelines

Platform

1. Be Respectful

Treat other people with respect, even when you disagree.

2. Keep Discussions Constructive

Keep comments and interactions meaningful, useful, and focused on the topic.

3. No Harassment

Do not repeatedly disturb, threaten, stalk, or intimidate another person.

4. No Hate or Discriminatory Content

Do not promote hatred, discrimination, or violence against people or groups.

5. Keep Personal Information Private

Do not publicly share another person's private or sensitive information without permission.

6. Comments

Keep comments relevant and avoid spam, flooding, and deliberate disruption.

7. Email Communication

Communicate respectfully and provide enough information for us to understand your request.

8. Don't Spam or Abuse the Platform

Do not intentionally overload, disrupt, or abuse platform features.

9. Don't Impersonate Others

Do not pretend to be another person or organization in a misleading way.

10. No Fraud or Malicious Activity

Do not use the platform for fraud, scams, phishing, malware, or other malicious activity.

11. Respect Copyright and Ownership

Only upload or share content that you have the right or authorization to use.

12. Keep Content Appropriate

Do not use the platform to distribute illegal or harmful content.

13. Reporting Problems

Report content or behavior that violates these guidelines through the available reporting channels.

14. Enforcement

Violations may result in warnings, restrictions, content removal, or other appropriate action.

15. Good-Faith Participation

You can criticize, question, disagree, and have difficult conversations respectfully.

16. Changes to These Guidelines

These guidelines may be updated as the platform develops and new features are introduced.

VJDY Direct

Consent Before Filming

Please make sure you are comfortable and have given your consent before being filmed or interviewed. If you don't want to be on camera or take part in an interview, just let us know.

Information Comes First

Make sure the information shared during the show is clear, accurate, and easy for everyone to understand. Important details should always be included when they are needed.

No Copyright Music Requests

Please don't request copyrighted music for the show. Unauthorized use of copyrighted music may result in the show being terminated or taken down.

Be Comfortable

Only participate when you feel comfortable. You don't have to share, speak, or do anything you're not comfortable with during the show.

Use Philippine Languages

VJDY Direct is primarily for conversations in Philippine languages. For now, please use English or Tagalog so everyone can follow along.

Optional Events and Occasions

Events and special occasions can be featured on VJDY Direct when available. Coverage is optional and may depend on the event, timing, and arrangements.

Interviews Are Optional

Interviews are completely optional. You can choose whether you'd like to participate when an interview is offered.

Wait for the Clapperboard

During an interview, please wait until the clapperboard has snapped before starting to speak. This helps us know when the official recording has started.

Check Availability

VJDY Direct coverage and interviews depend on availability. Please understand that we may not always be available for every event or occasion.

Moments

Highlights and Instagram

MOMENTS are shared as highlights and may also come from Instagram. Not every moment will be uploaded or featured.

Be Comfortable

Only share or take part in moments that you feel comfortable with. You can always choose not to be included.

No Sexual or Nude Content

Sexual content and nudity are not allowed in MOMENTS. Please keep shared moments appropriate and comfortable for everyone.

No Violent Scenes

Violent or disturbing scenes are not allowed in MOMENTS. Keep the content safe and suitable for the community.

Who May Appear

MOMENTS may feature people who are part of the event, activity, or moment being shared. If you're uncomfortable being featured, please let us know before the moment is recorded or shared.

How to Opt Out

If you don't want to appear in a MOMENT, simply let me know before capturing as soon as possible afterward. We'll do our best to make sure you're not included.

Music Store

Free to Use

The music available in the VJDY Music Store is free to use and open source. You can use it in your own projects, videos, streams, and other creative work.

Give Credit to AI Music

AI-generated music must be properly attributed when used. For MusicGPT, use <MusicGPT>, and for Suno, use <Suno> when giving credit.

Don't Claim the Music

Please don't upload or distribute the music while claiming it as your own copyright. You are welcome to use it, but the original work should still be credited properly.

Where You Can Use It

You can use the music in videos, livestreams, podcasts, games, personal projects, social media, and other creative work, as long as you follow the applicable attribution and usage rules.

TikTok Copyright Settings

When posting a video using music from the VJDY Music Store, sometimes copyright check is not perfect and will put your video taken down. check TikTok's copyright check settings and make sure the music is being used according to the provided usage terms. Do not claim the music as your own.

Instagram Reels

Highlighted music is also available through @vjdyofficialmusic on Instagram. You can use the available music for your Reels while following the music's attribution and usage guidelines.

YouTube Shorts Remix

For YouTube Shorts, you can use eligible VJDY music from the official Music Store channel through YouTube's Remix button when it is available. This lets you add the music directly to your Short.
