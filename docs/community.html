Community
Guidelines

Welcome to our community!

Our platform is built to help people share ideas, communicate, collaborate, and enjoy content together. These guidelines help keep interactions respectful, useful, and welcoming for everyone.

By using our platform, you agree to follow these guidelines when posting content, commenting, interacting with other users, or communicating with us through email.

1. Be Respectful

Treat other people with respect, even when you disagree.

Please avoid:

  • Personal attacks and insults
  • Harassment or bullying
  • Threats or intimidation
  • Discriminatory or hateful comments
  • Deliberately provoking or humiliating other users
  • Repeatedly targeting another person

Disagreement is allowed. Disrespect is not required.

2. Keep Discussions Constructive

Comments and interactions should contribute something meaningful to the conversation.

You are welcome to:

  • Ask questions
  • Share opinions
  • Disagree with others
  • Provide criticism
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Share experiences and suggestions

When criticizing something, focus on the content, idea, product, or topic, rather than attacking the person behind it.

3. No Harassment

Do not use the platform to repeatedly disturb, threaten, stalk, or intimidate another person.

This includes repeated unwanted contact through:

  • Comments
  • Direct interactions
  • Email
  • Other communication features provided by the platform

A person asking you to stop contacting them should be respected.

4. No Hate or Discriminatory Content

Do not promote hatred, discrimination, or violence against people based on protected or personal characteristics.

This includes content intended to degrade, dehumanize, threaten, or encourage hostility toward a group of people.

5. Keep Personal Information Private

Do not publicly share another person's private or sensitive information without their permission.

Examples include:

  • Home addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Private email addresses
  • Passwords
  • Authentication codes
  • Private conversations
  • Financial information
  • Other information that could reasonably put someone at risk

Do not use the platform to expose or encourage others to expose someone's private information.

6. Comments

Comments should be relevant to the content or discussion.

Avoid:

  • Spam
  • Repeated comments
  • Unrelated advertising
  • Excessive self-promotion
  • Comment flooding
  • Deliberate disruption of discussions

Constructive criticism and negative feedback are allowed. Criticism does not have to be positive to be acceptable.

7. Email Communication

When contacting us by email, please communicate respectfully and provide enough information for us to understand your request.

Please do not use email to:

  • Send threats
  • Harass staff or contributors
  • Send malicious files or links
  • Send excessive unsolicited messages
  • Impersonate another person or organization
  • Attempt to obtain another person's private information

If you are reporting an issue, providing relevant details such as the affected page, feature, error message, or approximate time can help us investigate it.

8. Don't Spam or Abuse the Platform

Do not intentionally overload, disrupt, or abuse platform features.

This includes:

  • Automated comment spam
  • Repeatedly submitting the same content
  • Creating accounts or content for abusive purposes
  • Attempting to bypass platform restrictions
  • Exploiting bugs in ways that disrupt other users
  • Using automated systems to generate excessive interactions

Legitimate automation or API use may be permitted where explicitly supported by the platform.

9. Don't Impersonate Others

Do not pretend to be another user, employee, contributor, organization, or public figure in a way that could reasonably mislead others.

Parody, fan accounts, and clearly fictional content should not be presented as official accounts.

10. No Fraud or Malicious Activity

Do not use the platform to facilitate fraud, scams, phishing, malware distribution, credential theft, or other malicious activity.

Do not attempt to trick users into giving you passwords, authentication codes, financial information, or other private information.

11. Respect Copyright and Ownership

Only upload or share content that you have the right to use.

Do not knowingly upload or distribute copyrighted, private, or otherwise restricted material without appropriate authorization.

If you believe content infringes your rights, please use the platform's designated reporting process.

12. Keep Content Appropriate

Do not use the platform to distribute content that is illegal or intended to cause harm.

Certain areas of the platform may have additional content rules depending on their purpose or audience.

13. Reporting Problems

If you encounter content or behavior that violates these guidelines, please report it through the available reporting mechanism or contact us by email.

When reporting something, providing the following information can help:

  • A link to the relevant content
  • The username or account involved
  • A description of the issue
  • Relevant screenshots or other evidence, when appropriate

Please do not retaliate against users who report problems.

14. Enforcement

We may take action when content or behavior violates these guidelines.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

  • Removing or restricting content
  • Removing comments
  • Limiting certain platform features
  • Issuing warnings
  • Temporarily restricting an account
  • Permanently restricting an account
  • Taking other appropriate measures

The response may depend on the severity, frequency, and circumstances of the violation.

We may also take action when behavior creates a significant risk to other users, the platform, or its operation.

15. Good-Faith Participation

These guidelines are intended to support healthy participation, not to prevent people from expressing different opinions.

You don't have to agree with everyone.

You don't have to like every piece of content.

You can criticize, question, disagree, and have difficult conversations.

The important part is how you interact with other people.

16. Changes to These Guidelines

We may update these guidelines as the platform develops and new features are introduced.

When significant changes are made, we may provide additional notice through the platform or other appropriate communication channels.

The Simple Rule

Be respectful. Be constructive. Protect people's privacy. Don't abuse the platform.

If you're unsure whether something is appropriate, consider whether you would be comfortable saying or sending it directly to another person in a normal conversation.

Thank you for helping make our community a welcoming and useful place.
